Why I Need This System - Personal Frustrations
The Reality of My Research Life
Lost Context Crisis:

12 cells with weeks of valuable data - I can't remember what any of it was for
A month since cells stopped testing - no idea why they stopped or what the next steps were
Screenshots from VersaStudio scattered everywhere - meaningless without context

Constant Interruptions:

Managing a team + other projects + planning meetings
Long electrochemical experiments (days/weeks) while my attention gets pulled elsewhere
Equipment conflicts forcing channel/temperature changes mid-experiment
Tech communications about moves/changes that I approve in passing

The Chaos Multiplier:

Multiple people using the same potentiostat
Everyone's insights locked in their individual memory
Data scattered across channels and conditions
Zero institutional memory - when people leave, knowledge disappears

The Existential Question
"What have I done all these experiments for?"
I have weeks of real electrochemical data, proper protocols, quality measurements - but the scientific value is trapped because I can't reconstruct:

What the original hypothesis was
Which cells were the promising ones
How this connects to product decisions
What I should do next

What I Need
A system that preserves research context across time and interruptions - so I never have to ask "What was I doing?" again.
Personal research continuity. That's it.
